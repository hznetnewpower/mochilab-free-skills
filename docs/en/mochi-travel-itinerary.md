# Timezone Itinerary Organizer

## What it helps with

Arrange user-selected itinerary stops, detect time overlaps and keep estimated costs separated by currency.

## Say it like this

After installation, describe the task in chat, paste the material or upload the relevant files.

**“Organize these selected itinerary stops, flag time overlaps and total CNY and USD budgets separately.”**

## What to provide

Provide dates, stops, start/end times, timezones and known costs, including confirmed booking details if useful.

No fixed request format is needed. The assistant asks a short follow-up when essential information is missing.

## What you receive

Receive an ordered itinerary, overlaps and separate currency totals.

The assistant explains results in plain language, provides actual output files where applicable and separates unresolved details.

## Scope

Current travel recommendations, opening hours, entry rules, fares and availability require live verification. User-supplied times are not verified transit times. No booking, payment or exchange-rate conversion occurs.
