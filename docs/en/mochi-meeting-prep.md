# Decision-Focused Meeting Prep

## What it helps with

Prepare a timed agenda, identify topics without a decision target and compare allocated time with the meeting budget.

## Say it like this

After installation, describe the task in chat, paste the material or upload the relevant files.

**“Prepare a 30-minute project review to decide the next milestone and discuss open questions. Propose timings and missing background.”**

## What to provide

Describe the objective, duration, topics and decisions needed.

No fixed request format is needed. The assistant asks a short follow-up when essential information is missing.

## What you receive

Receive a timed agenda and a preparation checklist.

The assistant explains results in plain language, provides actual output files where applicable and separates unresolved details.

## Scope

This prepares a meeting; it does not extract commitments from a past transcript, send invitations or fetch private attendee records. Cite any background facts used in the real briefing.


The lightweight installer includes the Skills assistant. Connect your Skills account in MochiBot Settings, then send:

```text
Install mochi-meeting-prep
```

For other deployments, first [connect the helper](https://mochilab.bz/Skills/Connect.aspx?lang=en). Do not provide keys in chat. Each workspace connects separately and authorized users may use the same membership account. This Skill is free; the local helper needs Python3.10+.
