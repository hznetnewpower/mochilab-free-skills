# Citation Normalizer

## What it helps with

Deduplicate user-supplied references by normalized DOI or title and export escaped BibTeX records.

## Say it like this

After installation, describe the task in chat, paste the material or upload the relevant files.

**“Deduplicate these references and export BibTeX, keeping meaningfully different editions separate.”**

## What to provide

Supply references or bibliographic files with existing DOIs and sources.

No fixed request format is needed. The assistant asks a short follow-up when essential information is missing.

## What you receive

Receive retained references, duplicate records and a BibTeX export.

The assistant explains results in plain language, provides actual output files where applicable and separates unresolved details.

## Scope

No DOI resolution, scholarly search or truth verification. Preserve distinct editions and corrections when bibliographically meaningful. Check exact publication metadata against the publisher before final citation.


The lightweight installer includes the Skills assistant. Connect your Skills account in MochiBot Settings, then send:

```text
Install mochi-citation-manager
```
