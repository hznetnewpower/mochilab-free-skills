# Customer Feedback Topic Map

## What it helps with

Apply user-defined keyword topic labels to selected feedback and expose unmatched records without inferring private traits.

## Say it like this

After installation, describe the task in chat, paste the material or upload the relevant files.

```text
Group this feedback by speed and billing. Use slow, lag and latency for speed, and invoice and charge for billing. Leave uncertain items visible.
```

## What to provide

Provide feedback and the desired topics, or ask for a proposed keyword scheme to review.

No fixed request format is needed. The assistant asks a short follow-up when essential information is missing.

## What you receive

Receive topic counts, source IDs and unmatched items.

The assistant explains results in plain language, provides actual output files where applicable and separates unresolved details.

## Scope

This is transparent keyword grouping, not sentiment analysis or semantic clustering. Review ambiguous examples and false positives before making product decisions. No customer reply or external upload occurs.


The lightweight installer includes the Skills assistant. Connect your Skills account in MochiBot Settings, then send:

```text
Install mochi-feedback-clusters
```
