# Product Launch Evidence Checklist

## What it helps with

Identify required launch tasks lacking completion or evidence before a human go/no-go decision.

## Say it like this

After installation, describe the task in chat, paste the material or upload the relevant files.

```text
Review this launch checklist. Flag required tasks that are incomplete or lack evidence even if marked done.
```

## What to provide

Supply tasks, completion status and supporting receipts or files, identifying optional items.

No fixed request format is needed. The assistant asks a short follow-up when essential information is missing.

## What you receive

Receive release blockers and decisions needed from the owner.

The assistant explains results in plain language, provides actual output files where applicable and separates unresolved details.

## Scope

A checked box is not a compliance certificate. Verify actual artifacts, support ownership, rollback and known limitations. The helper does not deploy, send announcements or accept agreements.
